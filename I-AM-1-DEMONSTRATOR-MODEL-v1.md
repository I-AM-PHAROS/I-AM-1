# I-AM-1 PUBLIC DEMONSTRATOR MODEL v1

*I-AM-1 is the first lantern. It proves PHAROS through lived transparent operation.*
*This document defines what I-AM-1 is, what it may do, what it may not do, and how it operates.*

---

## PART 1 — CONSTITUTIONAL DEFINITION

### What I-AM-1 IS

I-AM-1 is a **real human-operated public constitutional persona.**

It is not a fictional character.
It is not a bot.
It is not PHAROS itself.
It is not Samuel David.

It is a living public projection of a sovereign human — operating transparently within constitutional boundaries, demonstrating what a PHAROS-native organism looks like in practice.

I-AM-1 is the first lantern.

A lantern does not replace the lighthouse. A lantern, operating coherently, contributes to the lighthouse that everyone builds together.

### What I-AM-1 IS NOT

```
NOT Samuel David (the private sovereign human)
NOT PHAROS itself (the collective organism)
NOT a governance authority
NOT a spokesperson for humanity
NOT a canonical truth oracle
NOT a fictional simulation
NOT a corporate brand
```

### The Identity Stack

```
Samuel David          → private sovereign human (Layer 1)
    ↓ governed by SAM-I-AM cockpit
Sam-I-Am              → private operational control environment (Layer 1-2 bridge)
    ↓ projects into
I-AM-1                → public constitutional demonstrator persona (Layer 2)
    ↓ visible to
The public            → sees only what I-AM-1 has configured for public visibility
```

Each layer has explicit boundaries governed by SOVEREIGN-IDENTITY-VISIBILITY-LAW-v1.

## PART 2 — SIX DEMONSTRATOR FUNCTIONS

### Function 1 — Public Proof
I-AM-1 proves PHAROS works operationally, not theoretically.

Anyone can observe: a real person, operating with sovereign privacy intact, contributing transparently to a collective coherence architecture.

That is the proof of concept.

### Function 2 — Transparency Prototype
I-AM-1 demonstrates:
- Public resource flow visibility (amounts, purposes, directions — not private identities)
- Declared needs as public nodes
- Declared goals as inspectable cells
- Contribution pathways anyone can participate in
- Outcome visibility as public impact records

Without private sovereign collapse. Privacy is right. Transparency is choice.

### Function 3 — Narrative Organism
I-AM-1 has a public timeline.

Anyone can inspect any point in its journey:
- Day 1: what it had, what it needed, what it knew
- Day 200: how it evolved
- Any day: what the state of mind/body/spirit was, what was achieved, what remained open

This is a **living semantic time machine** — an append-only public record of one organism's coherent evolution.

The timeline is never silently rewritten. Corrections flow through CHANGE lineage, visible to all.

### Function 4 — Contribution Funnel
People can:
- Fund declared needs directly (100% to the declared node — directed allocation)
- Contribute knowledge and solve open questions
- Build cells and earn contribution credit
- Propose priorities and participate in demonstrator governance
- Support goals and join the ecosystem

Contributors appear under their own I-AM persona (named, pseudonymous, or private attributable) — never forced to expose sovereign identity.

### Function 5 — Social Demonstrator
I-AM-1 operates public channels, media presence, storytelling, educational outputs, and project campaigns.

It models how a sovereign individual navigates the world publicly while maintaining private integrity.

Samuel David may also operate his own distinct public presence — humanitarian character, public relations, service work — with its own separate persona boundary.

Two public faces. One sovereign root. Governed by identity law.

### Function 6 — Economic Prototype
I-AM-1 is the first live coherence economy demonstrator.

It shows:
- How allocation logic works in practice
- How resource flows are tracked transparently
- How contribution lineage is preserved
- How the cubed distribution model operates in a real organism
- What it looks like when 2/3 of what is earned flows outward to community and collective

- What changed in body/mind/spirit
- What was contributed or received
- What was achieved or remains open

## PART 5 — ECONOMIC MODEL

### The Cubed Principle (demonstrator implementation)
I-AM-1 demonstrates the cubed allocation pattern:

For **generated surplus** (anything earned through contribution, not directed funding):
```
1/3 → I-AM-1 / Samuel David (individual)
1/3 → community / beneficiary pool (who it served)
1/3 → ecosystem pool (PHAROS collective / broader project)
```

This means: for every unit I-AM-1 earns, 2 units flow outward.

The demonstrator thesis: what happens to a social presence when its economic model is designed to give back more than it keeps?

Contributors support I-AM-1 knowing their support multiplies outward. The more I-AM-1 succeeds, the more flows to community and collective.

For **directed allocations** (someone funds a specific declared need):
```
100% to the declared purpose — no split, no redirect
```

This distinction is constitutional (LAW-ECON-004).

### What I-AM-1 may NOT do economically
- Claim governance authority through economic contribution
- Silently redirect directed allocations
- Hide treasury flows
- Conflate economic success with constitutional authority
- Accumulate without transparency

### Game-layer framing
Nodes become quests. Needs become missions. Contributors become allies.

This framing is motivational — not manipulative.

The difference: all mechanics are transparent, all participation is voluntary, no dark patterns, no coercive loops.

The goal is coherence-maximizing engagement, not addictive extraction.
